01 / THE BUSINESS PLAN

Big tech.
Small pockets.

A little strategy. A little luck. A lot of “I should’ve sold those yesterday.” Inspired by John E. Dell’s Drug Wars, with a fresh cargo of silicon and AI absurdity.

[ BUY ]

Stuff the jacket.

GPUs, model weights, scraped Reddit data, AGI futures… Find a bargain and fill your pockets. Every lot takes one slot. Choose your baggage wisely.

[ JET ]

Chase the next market.

Hop between eight cities. Every flight costs a day and brings fresh prices. A deal in Silicon Valley might be somebody else’s expensive problem in New York.

[ SELL ]

Cash in. Or panic.

Headlines send prices soaring or tumbling. Surprise encounters can help or hurt. Sell at the right moment, grow your bankroll, and chase the local high score.

  1. Start a new game.

    Pick a name. Meet your jacket. You’re in Silicon Valley with $3,000 and 100 cargo slots.

  2. Find something worth carrying.

    Press B to buy, S to sell, and Space to open your jacket. Trading doesn’t use a day.

  3. Take your chances somewhere new.

    Press J to jet to another city. Watch the news, handle the surprises, and make those 30 days count.

04 / UNDER THE JACKET

Built with good company.

Want to build, tinker, or improve the chaos? The source is on GitHub. You’ll need Git, CMake 3.31+, Ninja, and a C++17 toolchain. The first build downloads dependencies, so building needs an internet connection.

Game & interface

LVGL · fmt · nlohmann/json · miniaudio

Fonts & images

FreeType · libpng · libjpeg-turbo · zlib

Platform & build

CardputerZero BSP / sysroot and an AArch64 Linux toolchain for the device. Desktop simulator builds also use SDL2. Device audio uses ALSA; system libraries and time zone data are supplied through the device packages.

See the build guide and dependency inventory for details. Based on the CardputerZero application template. The game’s Montserrat fonts use the SIL Open Font License 1.1; third-party components retain their own licenses.
